Privacy Policy

Learn how BRUSH and BID handles information and protects user privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We may store account, profile, artwork, bid, and shipping information needed to operate the auction platform.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, display profiles and artwork, process bids, administer auctions, and arrange delivery of winning artwork.

3. Data Protection

Reasonable technical and administrative measures should be used to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

4. Sharing

Information should only be shared when required to operate the service, complete delivery, comply with law, or with the user's consent.

5. Your Choices

Users can request correction of profile and shipping information through the relevant account pages.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated as the platform evolves.

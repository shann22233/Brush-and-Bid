How the weekly auction works

  1. One artwork is chosen Each week a single approved artwork is featured.
  2. Bidding opens The auction starts on the scheduled auction day.
  3. Bids stay open Raise your bid any time until the countdown ends.
  4. The highest bid wins The winner adds shipping details and completes payment.

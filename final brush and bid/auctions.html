How the Weekly Auction Works

1. One Artwork Every Week

Only one selected artwork is featured for auction during each weekly auction cycle.

2. Auction Opens on a Fixed Day

The artwork becomes available for bidding only on the scheduled auction day.

3. Bidding Is Open for 24 Hours

Bidders can place new bids or increase their existing bids during the 24-hour auction period.

4. Highest Bid Wins

When the 24-hour auction ends, the highest valid bid becomes the winning bid.

Terms & Conditions

Please read the following information carefully before using BRUSH and BID.

1. Account Use

Users must provide accurate information and keep their account credentials secure.

2. Artwork Submissions

Artists must submit original or lawfully owned artwork. Submissions are subject to administrative review.

3. Bidding

Bids are binding once submitted. A bidder should enter only amounts they are prepared to pay.

4. Auctions

Each weekly auction follows the published start and end time. The highest valid bid at closing is treated as the winning bid, subject to platform verification.

5. Prohibited Activity

Users must not manipulate bids, submit misleading information, or interfere with the operation of the platform.

6. Changes

BRUSH and BID may update these terms when necessary. Continued use after an update indicates acceptance.
